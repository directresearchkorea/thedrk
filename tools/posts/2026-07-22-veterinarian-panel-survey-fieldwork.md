# B2B Pet Market Research: Direct Fieldwork Insights from Korea's Veterinarian Survey

As the pet healthcare, veterinary medicine, and premium pet food industries undergo rapid professionalization, the demand for B2B veterinary market research in South Korea is growing significantly. However, due to veterinarians' demanding clinical schedules and limited contact points, managing fieldwork timelines for veterinarian surveys is notoriously challenging. Recently, Direct Research Korea (DRK) successfully completed an online quantitative survey targeting local veterinarians. Here, we share the practical fieldwork insights and strategies that allowed us to complete this project smoothly and without any scheduling delays.

![Dog and Cat in Veterinary Clinic](/insights/vets/dog_and_cat.jpg)

## 1. Securing Early Sample Pools Through a Proprietary Veterinarian Panel
The primary bottleneck in veterinary research is securing a sufficient sample size within tight deadlines. DRK resolves this by maintaining a proprietary panel of approximately 300 active, verified local veterinarians across South Korea. For this project, we leveraged our direct panel to generate a robust initial sample pool right from day one. This proactive approach removed the risk of recruitment delays and allowed us to complete the fieldwork comfortably ahead of schedule.

## 2. Flexible Scheduling and Systematic Panel Management
Because veterinary clinics operate with unpredictable schedules—often interrupted by emergency surgeries or patient surges—standard survey push methods fail. DRK utilized a flexible schedule management system, coordinating survey participation times around each veterinarian's clinic hours. By maintaining continuous communication and sending gentle, well-timed reminders, we systematically managed the participation rate. This ensured we secured stable, high-quality responses that aligned perfectly with our target quotas.

## 3. Maximizing Budget Efficiency via Direct Communication
Crucially, our direct contact model bypasses third-party recruiting agencies, reducing communication bottlenecks and eliminating redundant steps. By streamlining the recruitment process, we successfully cut out agency markups and middleman fees, significantly maximizing budget efficiency for our client. This allowed us to deliver high-quality, specialized B2B research data that perfectly met the client's expectations within their designated budget.
