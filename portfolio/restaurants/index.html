Japanese & Korean
Restaurants in Seoul

Fieldwork tracking, menu analysis, and dynamic data visualization dashboard.

Project Overview

This project involved comprehensive fieldwork visiting Japanese and Korean restaurants across Seoul on behalf of a Japanese food company. Our primary objectives were to survey current menu trends, analyze sales performance, review condiment usage, and conduct satisfaction surveys among key demographics.

To provide actionable insights, we mapped out the fieldwork routes and synthesized the collected data into the interactive dashboard below. This allows stakeholders to dynamically explore the locations, filter by restaurant type, and evaluate localized data points effectively.
