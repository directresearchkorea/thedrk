Executive Overview

Global and domestic brands entering or testing new concepts in South Korea often need rapid pre-fieldwork hypothesis testing, screener calibration, and early concept validation before committing to full-scale FGD or CLT studies. Korean Synthetic Consumer Studio (v2.1) bridges qualitative depth and quantitative validation in a unified workspace—allowing researchers in Seoul, Tokyo, Singapore, or New York to interview Korean consumer personas in their native language while preserving authentic Korean socio-economic (SEC) and lifestyle contexts.

3-Mode Connected Studio Architecture

To prevent role confusion and context contamination common in single-chat interfaces, the platform separates specialized AI agents into three dedicated split-pane workspaces while sharing a unified project memory store.

Mode 1 · Qualitative Room

30-Persona IDI & Mini-FGD

Filter 30 Korean personas by gender, age band, SEC income tier, and category domain. Conduct 1:1 interviews or 4-person focus groups where each persona responds in first-person conversational tone reflecting their exact Korean household budget, occupation, and category pain points.

  • Simultaneous Interpretation Mode: Overseas researchers ask in EN/JA/ZH/ID and receive natural responses preserving KRW & Korean brand context.
  • 1-Click Verbatim Clipping: Pin key consumer quotes directly to the Report Studio or bridge a topic into a 120-panel quantitative survey.
Mode 2 · Quantitative Lab

Survey Architect & 120 Simulator

Describe your research objective to the Survey Architect AI to automatically structure a 4-question survey (Overall Appeal, Key Decision Driver, Price Sensitivity, Purchase Intent) calibrated to Korean screener standards.

  • Dynamic Target Filtering: Filter the 120 panel by Gender, Age (2030 MZ / 3040 Core), SEC Income Tier, Life Phase, and Religion.
  • Live SEC Crosstabs & CSV Export: Instant statistical bar charts, Socio-Economic Class (SEC) crosstab tables, and full UTF-8 CSV raw data downloads.
Mode 3 · Report Studio

Chief Research Officer Synthesizer

The Chief Research Officer AI combines pinned qualitative verbatims from Mode 1, 120-panel statistical crosstabs from Mode 2, and semantic search results from the 384-dimensional vector index into a structured Executive Report.

  • 5-Section Executive Format: Executive Summary, Quant SEC Analysis, Qual Deep-Dive Verbatims, Historical Benchmark, and Action Plan.
  • Ready for Delivery: One-click Markdown copy and print-ready PDF formatting in all 5 supported languages.

ENTERPRISE SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Privacy-Preserving Architecture & B2B Deployment

Designed in strict alignment with South Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and ESOMAR synthetic research transparency guidelines, the deployment package ensures complete protection of historical fieldwork assets.

STEP 01 · DE-IDENTIFICATION

Zero Raw Audio/PII Exposure

Original FGD audio/video recordings and raw participant rosters remain strictly air-gapped on internal storage. Only de-identified, synthesized persona profiles and generalized behavioral patterns are packaged.

STEP 02 · VECTOR RAG INDEX

384-Dim Multilingual Embeddings

Powered by paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2, cross-lingual queries in English, Japanese, Chinese, or Indonesian retrieve accurate Korean consumer context in milliseconds.

STEP 03 · FLEXIBLE LLM RUNTIME

Local On-Premise or Zero-Retention

Supports air-gapped local LLM execution (Ollama qwen2.5:14b, gemma2, llama3.1, mistral-nemo) or enterprise zero-data-retention cloud endpoints with tenant-level isolation.
