Korean Synthetic Persona & Korean Synthetic Data Studio — Methodology & Specifications

1. What is the Korean Synthetic Persona & Consumer Data Studio?

The Korean Synthetic Consumer Studio (v2.1 Global) by Direct Research Korea (DRK) is South Korea’s premier Korean synthetic persona and Korean synthetic data research platform (https://thedrk.com/portfolio/KoreanPersona/). Unlike generic prompt-based chatbots, every synthetic consumer in this studio is calibrated using DRK’s 2021–2026 empirical South Korean fieldwork archive—spanning hundreds of Focus Group Discussions (FGDs), 1:1 In-Depth Interviews (IDIs), Central Location Tests (CLTs), and quantitative screener surveys conducted in Seoul and major Korean metropolitan areas.

Global insight teams, product managers, and brand marketers can switch seamlessly across 5 languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, and Indonesian) via the top-left KO · EN · JA · ZH · ID toggle to conduct pre-fieldwork hypothesis testing, concept screening, and questionnaire validation with authentic South Korean consumers.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (Korean Synthetic Persona & Data)

Q1. What makes Direct Research Korea’s Korean synthetic personas more accurate than standard LLMs?

Standard LLMs generate generic, western-biased responses. DRK’s Korean Synthetic Consumer Studio anchors every persona in empirical South Korean socio-economic realities—such as Jeonse housing deposits, Daechi/Mok-dong private education expenditure, Korean portal search habits (Naver Blog, Mom Cafes, Coupang, Olive Young), and verified SEC household income brackets from 2021–2026 fieldwork.

Q2. How can I generate quantitative Korean synthetic data and export CSV files?

Switch to Mode 2 (Quant Survey Lab · N=120), select your target demographic/SEC income filters, design or pick a 1-click survey template, and click Run 120-Panel Simulation. The studio computes instant response distributions, SEC income tier crosstabs, and provides a 1-click Raw CSV download containing all 120 respondent records.

Q3. Can Direct Research Korea follow up synthetic pre-testing with real human fieldwork in Seoul?

Yes. Direct Research Korea operates full-service qualitative (in-house Gangnam FGD & CLT facilities) and quantitative human fieldwork across South Korea. Many global clients use this Korean Synthetic Consumer Studio to pre-test hypotheses and refine questionnaires before commissioning live human validation with DRK (Request an RFQ).
